# r798B 评分者间信度（inter-rater reliability）· 逐字来源与判非

## 一、Cogn-IQ 选择系数与 kappa 悖论（全文逐字）

来源：https://cogn-iq.org/learn/theory/inter-rater-reliability/ （WebSearch 返回全文逐字）
- 加权 kappa："Weighted kappa repairs this by assigning each cell of the agreement table a weight for how far apart the two ratings are, so near-misses receive partial credit (Cohen, 1968). With the common quadratic weighting scheme, weighted kappa on ordered categories is closely related to the intraclass correlation"。
- Fleiss："Cohen's kappa is limited to exactly two raters… Fleiss' kappa generalizes the idea to any fixed number of raters (Fleiss, 1971)"。
- Krippendorff："it accommodates any number of raters, applies to nominal, ordinal, interval, or ratio data through a choice of difference function, and handles missing ratings gracefully, which has made it a standard in content analysis (Hayes & Krippendorff, 2007)"。
- ICC："the intraclass correlation coefficient (ICC), which expresses the proportion of total variance attributable to real differences between objects rather than to differences among raters (Shrout & Fleiss, 1979). The ICC is not one statistic but a family: the correct form depends on whether raters are treated as a random or fixed sample, whether agreement or mere consistency is wanted, and whether a single rating or an average of ratings will be used, and choosing the wrong form is a common reporting error (Koo & Li, 2016)。"
- 选择图（Figure 2）：nominal → Cohen's/Fleiss' kappa；ordered → weighted kappa 或 Krippendorff's alpha；continuous → ICC。
- 解释基准："Landis and Koch (1977)：>0.80 almost perfect, .61–.80 substantial, .41–.60 moderate, .21–.40 fair, <.21 slight to poor"；ICC 高风险决策常要求 >0.90。"these benchmarks should be used with care, because their authors chose the boundaries by convention rather than deriving them"。
- kappa 悖论："it can be low even when raters agree on almost every case… driven by prevalence. When one category is far more common than the others, the chance agreement pe that kappa subtracts becomes large… A rating task with 95% observed agreement can yield a kappa near zero if nearly all cases fall in one category."；第二悖论 unbalanced marginals。"kappa should never be reported alone — the raw percent agreement and the marginal distributions belong alongside it"。

## 二、MCP Analytics 决策指南与样本量（全文逐字）

来源：https://mcpanalytics.ai/articles/cohens-kappa-agreement-beyond-chance （全文逐字）
- 公式：kappa = (observed agreement − expected agreement) / (1 − expected agreement)，expected 由各 rater 边际分布算出，范围 −1 到 1。
- 决策指南原文：Weighted Kappa 用于有序类目（Likert/severity）；Fleiss' Kappa 用于 ≥3 rater 且全部评全部；Krippendorff's Alpha 用于 ≥3 rater、不同子集评不同 item、缺失数据、跨 nominal/ordinal/interval/ratio；ICC 用于连续数值（consistency 或 absolute agreement）；Percent Agreement 仅 pilot 快速 sanity check 且须补 kappa。
- 机会一致例："If 90% of your cases are negative, two raters who each guess 'negative' 90% of the time, independently of the case, will agree 82% of the time (0.9×0.9 + 0.1×0.1) with zero actual skill. Kappa corrects for this chance agreement."
- 负 kappa："raters agree less often than random chance would predict… often indicates: reversed coding schemes, fundamental misunderstanding of categories, or one rater systematically inverting judgments. Investigate immediately。"
- 样本量："for two balanced categories and kappa around 0.6, a 95% CI of ±0.1 needs about 246 observations."；"lower agreement requires larger samples"。
- "Don't average pairwise Cohen's kappa values. This ignores the correlation structure and gives misleading results."

## 三、PaloAltoHealthLabs 标签形状选系数表 + ppos/pneg（全文逐字）

来源：https://paloaltohealthlabs.com/resources/inter-rater-agreement-medical-data-labeling.html （全文逐字）
- 表：Percent agreement（any/2+，忽略机会一致）；Cohen's kappa（Nominal，2）；Weighted kappa（Ordinal，2，结果依赖 weighting scheme 须报告）；Fleiss' kappa（Nominal，>2，同流行率敏感）；Krippendorff's alpha（Nominal–ratio，2+，须报告 data level）；ICC（Continuous，2+，10 种形式须报 model/type/definition）；Dice/IoU（分割 mask，pairwise，小结构受少量像素差惩罚大）；Event-level F1（时序事件如 EEG 癫痫，pairwise，须定义 matching rule: any-overlap 或 time-aligned）。
- "Pick the metric from the shape of the label, not from habit."；"Reliability is a precondition for validity, never evidence of it."（引 CASRAI）
- Koo & Li 10 种 ICC："values less than 0.5, between 0.5 and 0.75, between 0.75 and 0.9, and greater than 0.90 are indicative of poor, moderate, good, and excellent reliability"。
- kappa 悖论修补："the omnibus value of kappa should always be accompanied by separate individual values of ppos and pneg…"（Feinstein & Cicchetti 1990）。
- "a score as low as 0.41 might be acceptable"（McHugh 2012 批 Cohen 解释对健康研究过宽松）。

## 四、CASRAI 信度≠效度 + 阈值约定 + Gwet AC1（全文逐字）

来源：https://casrai.org/guides/inter-rater-reliability-choosing-a-coefficient （全文逐字）
- "High inter-rater reliability means raters apply your coding scheme consistently. It does not mean the scheme measures what you intended, and it does not mean the ratings are accurate — two raters trained on the same wrong definition will agree beautifully. Reliability is a precondition for validity, never evidence of it."
- 信度转个体 MDC 是另一步："converting reliability into a standard error of measurement and then a minimal detectable change threshold is a separate step."
- 95% 一致率三情形："95% agreement produces kappas of 0.90, 0.26 and −0.02 depending only on prevalence."；Gwet's AC1 抗偏但机会定义有争议，"Use it alongside kappa and raw agreement, pre-specified in your protocol — not as a substitute chosen after kappa disappointed you."
- "Landis & Koch bands (0.61–0.80 'substantial', above 0.81 'almost perfect') are the usual reference for kappa and Krippendorff recommends α ≥ 0.800… both conventions rather than derived thresholds. The defensible practice is to pre-specify a threshold justified by what the rating will be used for, and to judge the result from its confidence interval."
- "Cohen's kappa is defined for exactly two raters. Fleiss' kappa handles three or more… With three fixed raters, reporting the pairwise Cohen's kappas as well as an overall Fleiss' kappa is more diagnostic, because it reveals a single divergent rater that an aggregate figure conceals."
- "ICC for categorical data, or kappa for continuous scores? No in both directions — each is a category error. The one legitimate bridge is that quadratically weighted kappa on an ordered scale is, under standard conditions, numerically equivalent to a two-way ICC。"

## 五、Quali-Fi 低一致三因 + 工作流（全文逐字）

来源：https://quali-fi.com/learn/inter-rater-reliability （全文逐字）
- "Low reliability usually signals one of three problems: Vague code definitions… Overlapping categories… Insufficient training…"
- 工作流：train coders → pilot independently（10–20% 数据，最少 30–50 例）→ calculate reliability on pilot → 低于阈值则查分歧/澄清定义/再 pilot → 达标后全量 → 项目期周期性复核 → document（指标、双评例数、code frame 版本）。
- "If your coded data depends on who happened to do the coding, it's not reliable data, it's one person's opinion."
