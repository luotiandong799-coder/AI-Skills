# r791C · 蝴蝶结分析（bow tie）全文（RI · 2026-11-22 实拉）

## 本体与要素
蝴蝶结把一条风险路径沿单一「顶事件」左右展开：hazard（受控态的风险源）→ 左 threats（可触发顶事件的原因）+ prevention barriers（挡在威胁与顶事件之间）→ 顶事件（对危害失去控制）→ 右 consequences（屏障失败后的非预期后果）+ mitigation barriers（限制/减轻后果），屏障外侧再挂 degradation factors（削弱屏障的条件）与 degradation controls（保护屏障不被削弱）。源自 ICI 1970s，CCPS + Energy Institute 2018《Bow Ties in Risk Management》统一术语（IChemE Hazards 28 论文，作者来自 BP / ABS Group / CCPS / EI）。qhsetech 逐字："A bow tie shows pathways and barriers around a single event; it is not a calculation ... it does not itself give a frequency."

## 三条可机检独点
1. **与 FTA/ETA 分工**：qhsetech 逐字「A fault tree works backwards from an event to the combinations of causes that could produce it and is often used to estimate likelihood, while a risk matrix scores a hazard on severity and likelihood. A bow tie can show where the controls are ... but it does not itself give a frequency.」⇒ 蝴蝶结把 §r779C FTA（演绎致祸组合）/ETA（归纳触发走向）的屏障直接挂到路径上并可记状态，自身不产生频率；RI 复盘用它回答「哪几道屏障本该挡住、状态如何」，不用它算概率。
2. **真屏障三门 + 退化控制**：qhsetech 逐字「barriers should be effective, independent and auditable, which ... typically leaves between one and five barriers on each threat pathway」「long lists of supposed barriers can give a false sense of security」「an equipment item such as a shutdown valve is only part of a barrier, which also needs detection and decision elements」「training, audit, management of change and contractor management ... are not barriers in themselves」；ORS 逐字「A barrier must have the capability on its own to prevent or mitigate a bow tie sequence and meet traditional LOPA requirements for Independent Protection Layers (IPLs)」「Active barriers must include all elements of detect-decide-act or, in functional safety terms: sensor-logic-final element」「The use of "Human error" as a threat ... is generally NOT recommended as this is better treated as degradation factors」「Threats are not barrier failures, which is a common mistake made」。⇒ 复盘写「加培训/加审批」先判它在图上的位置：多为退化控制或管理体系，不是真屏障；屏障清单超过 5 条需重审独立性。
3. **近失改屏障状态而非只重训**：qhsetech 逐字「When a near miss shows a load carried over an unmarked aisle, the reviewer does not simply retrain the crew. The diagram shows that the exclusion-zone barrier failed, so the record marks it degraded, the actions address how zones are set in the lift plan, and a later verification returns it to verified status.」+ ICMM 关键控制需「clear performance standards, defined ownership and regular verification」；记录要素含 barrier owner / performance standard / status(verified,due,degraded,failed) / 最后核验日期与证据。⇒ 与 §r751C 三态结论、§r779C 共因失效呼应，但落点在「屏障作为可跟踪对象」而非抽象结论。
