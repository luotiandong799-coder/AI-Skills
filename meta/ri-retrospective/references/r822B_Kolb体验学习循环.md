# r822B · Kolb 体验学习循环（RI · 2026-11-28）

> 主题：Experiential Learning Theory (ELT) / 四阶段学习循环。与 §r767C AAR 四问（事后回顾流程）、§r759C 成功失败同源（Hollnagel 弹性）、§r771C 单双三环（学习深度分级）异命题——本章管「经验如何经过四阶段转化为知识，以及为何复盘必须走完整循环」。
> 实拉：Kolb 官方机构 Experience Based Learning Systems（EBLS）两页 + Emerald 同行评审期刊（WebSearch 快照逐字），每轮 ≥2 独立域一手。

## 一、定义与四阶段（EBLS 官方逐字）

> "Learning is defined as 'the process whereby knowledge is created through the transformation of experience. Knowledge results from the combination of grasping and transforming experience.' (Kolb, 1984, p. 41)"

**两对辩证模态**（KELP 技术规范逐字）：
- 把握经验（grasping）：**Concrete Experience (CE)** ↔ **Abstract Conceptualization (AC)**
- 转化经验（transforming）：**Reflective Observation (RO)** ↔ **Active Experimentation (AE)**
- "Learning arises from the resolution of creative tension among these four learning modes."
- "an idealized learning cycle or spiral where the learner 'touches all the bases'—experiencing (CE), reflecting (RO), thinking (AC), and acting (AE)—in a recursive process that is sensitive to the learning situation and what is being learned."
- 链条：即时/具体经验 → 观察与反思 → 提炼为抽象概念 → 从中推出行动含义 → 主动检验 → 成为新经验的指引。

工程类比（agent 复盘落地）：CE=本次任务真实经过 → RO=逐条对照预期与实际 → AC=写出可复用的规则/判据 → AE=下轮任务中实测该规则 → 回环。只做 CE+RO 是「有感受无沉淀」，只做 AC+AE 是「套公式不校准」。

## 二、八个关键洞察（EBLS 官方《Eight Important Things》逐字）

1. **"Learning is an endlessly recurring cycle not a linear process."** 不是线性信息传递；"learning is like breathing; a lifelong process of taking in and putting out"；对应 Freire 的 "banking concept of education" 之反面。
2. **"Experiencing is necessary for learning."** 教育者提供 direct concrete experiencing event（field trip / lab experiment / role play），再组织个人或小组反思；概念化阶段理解经验意义（常配讲座或阅读），最后要求学员在生活与工作中应用。
3. **"The brain is built for experiential learning."**
4. **"The dialectic poles of the cycle are what motivate learning."** 四模态间的创造性张力是学习驱动力。
5. **"Learning styles are different ways of going around the learning cycle."** 学习风格=绕行的不同路径，非固定能力标签。
6. **"Full cycle learning increases learning flexibility and development."** 走完整循环提升灵活度与发展。
7. **"Teaching around the learning cycle."**
8. **"The learning cycle can be a rubric for holistic, authentic assessment."**

EBLS 明确："The simplicity and usefulness of the four stage cycle of experiencing, reflecting, thinking and acting is the main reason for its popularity"；并强调循环是 "recursive circle or spiral as opposed to the linear, traditional information transmission model"。课程设计启示："Organise your course or curriculum as a series of learning cycles to form a deepening spiral of learning that expands in complexity and application."

## 三、ELT 六条命题（Evin Gencel & Erdogan & Kolb & Kolb, Rubric for Experiential Training, DOI 10.29329/ijpe.2021.366.12，WebFetch 200 摘要逐字）

- "Learning should be conceived as a process and not as outcomes."
- "As a matter of fact, all learning is re-learning." 学习者的想法可被检验、测试、与新想法整合。
- "Learning process includes differences such as ideas, reflections, and problem-solving styles of the learners."
- "Learning is a holistic process involving experiencing, reflecting, thinking, and acting for consistency purpose."
- "Learning occurs in a way that individuals assimilate new experiences and adapt these to the concepts gained through previous experiences and associate these concepts with new experiences."
- "Learning is based on the constructivist theory explaining that the learner creates 'learning', but not on the traditional teaching focusing on transferring previously known and immutable ideas."

理论源流（同页逐字）："studies of James, Dewey, Follet, Lewin, Piaget, Vygotsky, Jung, Rogers and Freire ... were theorized by Kolb in a holistic and concrete model in 1984."

## 四、第二独立域：Emerald 同行评审交叉（WebSearch 快照逐字）

《Students' perspectives on an internship experience》(Emerald, DOI 10.1108/JRIT-07-2024-0198)：
> "Experiential education has a rich history and has been associated with scholars like Dewey, Lewin, Joplin and Kolb ... Kolb further added to the experiential learning phenomenon by introducing four crucial phases to the process of experiential learning, including concrete experiences, reflective observation, abstract conceptualization and active experimentation."

同页追溯：Dewey 反传统、创 learner-centered approach，"students learn better when they experience learning, which can lead to reflective thought and action"；Lewin 聚焦评估体验学习方法；Joplin 提出五阶段模型。独立佐证四阶段的学术承认度与源流分野。

## 五、神经科学映射与边界（EBLS KELP 技术规范逐字，标推断）

Zull（2002/2011）提出 ELT 与脑结构对应："concrete experiences come through the sensory cortex, reflective observation involves the integrative cortex at the back, creating new abstract concepts occurs in the frontal integrative cortex, and active testing involves the motor brain." 该映射为单一学者提议、非 ELT 核心公理；agent 场景无可直接套用的神经层证据，按 rules/08 §五标**推断**，不作为记忆写入依据。
