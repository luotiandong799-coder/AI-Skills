# r822C · 生成效应（Generation Effect）（MI · 2026-11-28）

> 主题：自己生成的材料比单纯阅读的材料记得更好。与 §r806C 双重编码（表象/言语双码）、§r798A 自我参照效应（挂「关于我」的组织）、§r751A 检索即改写（取回打开可写窗口）异命题——本章管**编码阶段「生成 vs 阅读」这一操作本身带来的记忆增益及其边界**。
> 实拉：ScienceDirect 同行评审论文两篇（Chen/Kalyuga/Sweller 2016；McDaniel/Einstein 1988）+ Graf 1982 + APA 专刊（WebFetch 200 + WebSearch 快照逐字），每轮 ≥2 独立域一手。

## 一、经典范式与定义（ScienceDirect 2016 逐字）

> "The generation effect occurs when items that are generated based on a given stimulus and an encoding rule are better remembered than the same items which are simply read (Slamecka & Graf, 1978)."

配对联想词表范式："one group may be asked to read a paired associate list consisting of words and their opposites (e.g., hot-cold) while the other group may be presented the first word of each pair and told to generate the second word." 生成组对目标词的记忆优于阅读组。

## 二、边界条件一：元素交互性 + 专长（Chen, Kalyuga & Sweller, Learning and Instruction 45 (2016) 20–30，WebFetch 200 全文逐字）

研究背景矛盾：worked example effect（高指导组学得更好）vs generation effect（低指导/自己生成组学得更好）。
> "The contradiction ... can be resolved by the suggestion that the worked example effect is obtained using materials high in element interactivity, whereas simpler, low element interactivity materials result in the generation effect."

Highlights 逐字：
- "The worked example effect relies on high element interactivity materials."
- "The generation effect requires materials low in element interactivity."
- "Both effects may be more likely to be found on a delayed rather than an immediate test."

2(guidance: low/high) × 2(element interactivity: low/high) × 2(expertise: low/high) 实验，高中三角函数：
- 即时测验：新手出现 worked example effect（高指导优），知识较多者出现 generation effect。
- 延迟测验：三因素交互——新手在高元素交互材料上是 worked example effect、低元素交互材料上是 generation effect；知识较多者在低**和**高元素交互材料上都是 generation effect。

理论链接：generation effect 与 **expertise reversal effect** 相关（"the effectiveness of instructional procedures reverses with increasing expertise", Kalyuga et al., 2003）。同一材料对新手高交互、对专家低交互 → 专家更容易从生成中获益。**落地含义：不能把「让自己生成」当普适学习法；材料复杂且使用者尚不熟时，先给示例（worked example）优于逼其生成。**

## 三、边界条件二：被试间设计会削弱/消失（McDaniel & Einstein, JML 1988，WebSearch 快照逐字）

论文《A contextual account of the generation effect: A three-factor theory》摘要逐字：
> "Recent studies have shown that the generation effect (items generated by an individual are better remembered than items that are read) can be diminished or eliminated under between-subjects deployment of the encoding task (generating vs. reading)."

三实验：
- Exp 1&2（结构化词表）：free recall 只在特定 cue-target 条件与 written-response 条件下有显著 generation effect；recognition 和 cued recall 在所有条件下都有。
- Exp 3（intentional learning instructions）：**未获显著 generation effect**。
- 理论提议："generation enhances both cue-target relational processing and individual-item processing; namely, in some contexts generation will enhance whole-list processing."（三因素：关系加工、单项加工、整表加工）

**落地含义：生成效应的稳健性依赖测验类型（识别/线索回忆 > 自由回忆）与指导语（偶然学习 > 刻意学习），不可无条件外推。**

## 四、机制旁证：distinctiveness / production effect（Graf 1982；APA 2016 专刊逐字）

- Graf《The memorial consequences of generation and transformation》(JVLVB 1982)：自生成句子 vs 阅读句子、正常朝向 vs 旋转朝向阅读；两者都提升词识别，且只在**测验时 reinstate 学习语境**时对有意义的句子更强 → 生成与旋转阅读共享精细加工与整合过程。
- APA《The Production Effect in Memory》专刊（Canadian J Exp Psych 70(2), 2016）：production effect = 出声读比默读记得好（MacLeod et al., 2010）；机制两说——**distinctiveness heuristic**（"individuals might use the recollection of the study episode of an item as the evidence needed that the item was studied", Dodson & Schacter 2001）与 **strength-based account**（"reading words aloud strengthens the representations of those items more so than does reading words silently"）。边界："Production Does Not Improve Memory for Face–Name Associations"（Hourihan & Smith）→ 生成/产出类增益并非跨材料普适。
- Begg et al. (1989)《The generation effect is no artifact: Generating makes words distinctive》为 distinctiveness 说提供直接证据；Burns (1990) 单/多因素之争、Karpicke & Zaromb (2010) 区分 testing effect 与 generation effect（检索模式不同）均列为**机制未定论**，不强行统一。

## 五、agent 落地规则（推断，按 rules/08 §五标注）

1. **写入侧**：需要长期记住的判据/规则，让 agent 先**自己生成表述**（如「用一句话写出这条规则的适用边界」）再核对原文，而不是只复制粘贴原文；对应低元素交互、可生成的目标。
2. **不适用**：规则本身高度复杂、agent 尚未掌握（高元素交互）→ 先给 worked example（完整范例）让其对照，强行生成会引入错误并增 extraneous load。
3. **测验侧**：验收用线索回忆/识别式提问比自由回忆更稳；刻意「我要记住它」的指导语可能消除增益，改为任务驱动的自然生成。
4. **边界**：文献来自人类被试词表与课堂材料，向 agent 记忆写入的迁移是**推断**，非实证结论；不得当作已验证规则写进用户级 MEMORY.md。
