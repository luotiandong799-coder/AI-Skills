# r798A 自我参照效应（self-reference effect）· 逐字来源与判非

## 一、Craik & Tulving 加工深度与 Rogers 1977 原始范式（Sage 教材全文逐字）

来源：https://in.sagepub.com/sites/default/files/upm-assets/66302_book_item_66302.pdf （《Fundamentals of Cognitive Psychology》第5章 Remembering Events，WebSearch 返回全文逐字）

逐字要点：
- "visual and acoustic encoding is inferior to semantic encoding on memory tests (Craik & Lockhart, 1972)"，语义编码产生更 elaborate 表征支持更好回忆。
- "processing the information in relation to our self-concept is superior, a finding called the self-reference effect. Rogers, Kuiper, and Kirker (1977) found that when people asked whether a word applied to themselves (e.g., ambitious), later recall rose above that obtained for even the semantic-orienting task."
- Figure 5.2 Recall as a function of the level of processing（adapted from Rogers, Kuiper, & Kirker 1977）四类：Physical / Acoustic / Semantic / Self-Reference，平均回忆词数依次约 2.5 / 1.5 / 1 / 3 量级——self-reference 最高（图示数值以教材图为准）。
- 消费者广告语境同样成立："Have you ever used this product?" 支持比语义定向更好的品牌回忆（D’Ydewalle et al. 1985）。
- 关键成分："relating the information to highly developed representations in long-term memory, providing many links with well-ingrained information (Bellezza, 1986)"。
- 生存加工对照："Nairne, Thompson, and Pandeirada (2007) proposed that long-term memory may have evolved… to retain information relevant to survival"，survival-orienting task 优于 pleasantness 与 self-reference task。
- Transfer-appropriate processing："Morris, Bransford, and Franks (1977) found that the typical levels of processing effect can be reversed by picking a test that is appropriate to visual- or acoustic-orienting tasks"——编码与检索重叠比编码本身更重要。

## 二、cognitivepsychology.com 机制与元分析（全文逐字）

来源：https://cognitivepsychology.com/Self_Reference_Effect （全文逐字）

逐字要点：
- "first systematically documented by Rogers et al. (1977), shows that asking whether information describes oneself produces superior recall compared to other encoding tasks, including general semantic processing."
- Rogers 范式四任务：structural（"Is it printed in big letters?"）/ phonemic（"Does it rhyme with 'train'?"）/ semantic（"Does it mean the same as 'friendly'?"）/ self-referential（"Does it describe you?"）；"Self-referenced words achieved 30% recall compared to 13% for semantic processing, 7% for phonemic, and 3% for structural"。
- 四机制：自我概念是广泛发展的知识结构（vast network of associations → retrieval cues）；elaboration（自发生成个人例子/情绪连接）；组织收益（self-schema 归类聚类）；情绪显著性（amygdala-hippocampal 巩固）。
- "Symons and Johnson's (1997) meta-analysis of 129 studies confirmed the effect's robustness across materials, populations, and retention intervals."
- 神经：mPFC（尤其 ventromedial）在 self-reference 任务下活动高于 other-reference/semantic，且预测后续记忆；海马绑定 self-related features，后扣带（默认网络）整合自传体记忆；mPFC-海马功能连接预测记忆强度。
- 发展与文化：约 5–6 岁出现，随自我概念发展增强；老年人其他记忆衰退时 SRE 仍稳健（补偿策略）；文化调节效应量。

## 三、自我参照 vs 生存加工之争（ScienceDirect 两篇全文逐字）

来源1：https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1053810012002395 （《Survival of the selfish: Contrasting self-referential and survival-based encoding》，全文逐字可见段落）
- "processing personal survival is clearly a self-referential encoding context"（Nairne 2007 自认）；Klein 2012："few things are more self-relevant than one's own survival"。
- SRE 机制："self-referencing promotes better organization in memory, and leads to relatively rich representations due to elaboration by the detailed and accessible self-concept (Klein and Kihlstrom, 1986…)"。
- Nairne 的 self-referencing 任务被 Klein 指可能失败（要求评"引发自传体记忆的可能性"而非真回忆）；Klein 用标准自参照指令复制后 "the memory advantage for survival-based over self-referential processing was rendered non-significant"。
- Weinstein 2008 第一/第三人称（self/friend/stranger survival）效应相近；Kang 2008 卡通角色生存语境仍增强记忆——但均为 between-groups，可能受"若是我"的自我投射污染。

来源2：https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0749596X17300037 （《Survival processing versus self-reference: A memory advantage following descriptive self-referential encoding》，全文逐字可见段落）
- "the descriptive self-reference task is more effective than the autobiographical self-reference task"（Klein et al. 综述区分两类自参照任务）；本研究用 Rogers 1977 descriptive task 对比 survival。
- 实验用 Anderson 1968 特质形容词；90 名本科生；between-subjects 设计避免 carry-over（"within-subject designs are problematic for the self-reference effect because of the danger of carry-over effects" — Conway et al. 2001）。
- Experiment 1 结果："self-referential encoding led to higher levels of recall than survival processing"。

来源3：https://opentext.wsu.edu/psych105/?p=237 （WSU 开放教材，全文逐字）
- Craik & Tulving 1975 三级加工（visual 字体 / acoustic 押韵 / semantic 意义），语义回忆最优。
- "The self-reference effect represents a tendency for an individual to have better memory for information that relates to oneself in comparison to material that has less personal relevance (Rogers, Kuiper & Kirker, 1977)."
- Generation effect："information you generate or create is more likely to be accurately recalled compared to information you heard or read (Slameka & Graf, 1978)"——与 SRE 正交的另一编码增强机制。
