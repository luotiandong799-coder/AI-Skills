## 独点 1：证伪/证实的逻辑不对称 + corroboration 不是 verified
- SEP Popper §3 逐字：「it is logically impossible to verify a universal proposition by reference to experience (as Hume saw clearly), but a single genuine counter-instance falsifies the corresponding universal law. In a word, an exception, far from 'proving' a rule, conclusively refutes it.」
- 「'All A are X' is equivalent to 'No A is not-X' which is falsified if 'Some A is not-X' turns out to be true.」——普遍定律是禁止性（prohibitive）陈述，一个反例即逻辑证伪。
- 「corroboration ... should count scientifically only if it is the positive result of a genuinely 'risky' prediction, which might conceivably have been false.」——支持性证据只有来自「本可能为假」的冒险预测才算数；「it is easy ... to obtain evidence in favour of virtually any theory」故堆正面证据无划界力。
- §5 四步检验（2002:9）：(a) formal 内部一致性无矛盾；(b) semi-formal 公理化区分经验/逻辑元素、排除 tautological；(c) 与既有理论比解释力与预测力——「a theory X is better than a 'rival' theory Y if X has greater empirical content, and hence greater predictive power」；(d) 经验应用检验——「If such conclusions are shown to be true, the theory is corroborated (but never verified). If ... false ... logically the theory is falsified ... He does not, however, abandon the present theory until such time as he has a better one to substitute for it.」
- §6：「the more improbable a theory is the better it is scientifically, because the probability and informative content of a theory vary inversely」「Informative content ... is in direct proportion to testability.」——高信息量=低概率=高可检验性，科学要的是「接近真但概率低」的强断言，不是高概率的同义反复。
- 划界：「if a theory is incompatible with possible empirical observations it is scientific; conversely, a theory which is compatible with all such observations ... is unscientific.」Psychoanalysis（与一切观察相容）与 Einstein（light deflection 若假即整个理论假、1919 Eddington 证实）对照。
- 方法论与逻辑分层（§4）：逻辑上「a universal statement is falsified by a single genuine counter-instance」；方法上「a single conflicting ... counter-instance is never sufficient methodologically for falsification」因观察偏差/测量误差可使结果只是表面不相容 ⇒ 「a theory that has withstood rigorous testing ... may be retained provisionally as the best available theory until it is finally falsified and/or is superseded by a better theory.」
-  tetradic schema（§7，1979:287）：`P1 → TT → EE → P2`（初始问题→试探性理论→试错消除→新问题）。「all knowledge is provisional, conjectural, hypothetical—the universal theories of science can never be conclusively established.」

## 独点 2：Duhem-Quine 整体论——无孤立检验、无 crucial experiment、同一策略两结果
- SEP Underdetermination §1 两型区分：**holist**「our inability to test hypotheses in isolation leaves us underdetermined in our response to a failed prediction」（失败后改哪个信念不确定）；**contrastive**「for any body of evidence confirming a theory, there might well be other theories that are also well confirmed by that very same body of evidence」（同一证据支持多个理论）。两者正交：holist 从理论出发问「怎么改」，contrastive 从证据出发问「哪个理论」。
- Duhem 1914/1954,185 逐字：「the physicist can never subject an isolated hypothesis to experimental test, but only a whole group of hypotheses; when the experiment is in disagreement with his predictions, what he learns is that at least one of the hypotheses constituting this group is unacceptable and ought to be modified; but the experiment does not designate which one should be changed.」——失败检验只给「至少一处错」，不指认错在哪。
- 「there cannot be any such thing as a 'crucial experiment' (experimentum crucis): a single experiment whose outcome is predicted differently by two competing theories and which therefore serves to definitively confirm one and refute the other.」Foucault 光速介质实验"否定"光的微粒说：Duhem 指出实验判定的只是 Newton-Laplace-Biot 整组命题有错，「does not tell us where the error lies」，微粒说仍可与实验协调。
- §3.1 对比型：「Do two hypotheses in physics ever constitute such a strict dilemma? Shall we ever dare to assert that no other hypothesis is imaginable?」——几何两矛盾定理无第三判断，物理两假说从不构成这种严格二择。
- **同一保护策略两种结果**（§2.1）：Newton 天体力学预测天王星轨道失败→科学家不弃理论而质疑「太阳系只有七行星」的背景假设，Adams/Leverrier 算出第八颗行星位置→1846 发现海王星（策略成功，尽管 Newton 理论本身是错的）；同一策略用于水星近日点进动、假设「Vulcan」火神星→失败，直到广义相对论才解释。「by no means a foregone conclusion which member of such a collection should be abandoned or revised in response to a failed empirical test」——要不要改辅助假设不能一刀切，要看后续是否真产出新预测。
- Quine 1951,42-3 逐字：「The totality of our so-called knowledge or beliefs ... is a man-made fabric which impinges on experience only along the edges ... A conflict with experience at the periphery occasions readjustments in the interior of the field. But the total field is so underdetermined by its boundary conditions, experience, that there is much latitude of choice as to what statements to reevaluate ... No particular experiences are linked with any particular statements in the interior ...」「The unit of empirical significance is the whole of science.」
- 即便「Elm Street 有砖房」这种信念也能「come what may」通过别处激进调整保住⇒ 原则上可修改逻辑/数学/词义；唯一检验是「fits into a web of connected beliefs that accords well with our experience on the whole.」
- 修正的实际选择靠 **pragmatic virtues**（1955）：「simplicity, familiarity, scope, and fecundity, along with conformity to experience」+ conservatism「the smallest possible number of changes to the least central beliefs」= minimal mutilation；「'closer' to the sensory periphery ... simply that we are more likely to revise the first than the second」——中心/边缘只是「愿不愿改」的心理度量。「at bottom, is what evidence is」(1955,251)。

## 独点 3：ad hoc 免疫与 Popper 晚期立场收缩——判别在「修改的动机」不在「是否修改」
- SEP Popper §2：Marxism 起初真预测（故科学），预测落空后「saved from falsification by the addition of ad hoc hypotheses which made it compatible with the facts」→ 退化为 pseudo-scientific dogma；Hegel 辩证法被用作「reinforced dogmatism」(1963:334) 通过消除矛盾证据的可能来容纳教条。
- §12 Critical Evaluation 关键收缩（必须如实写，不能只讲早期强版本）：「Popper's final position is that he acknowledges that it is impossible to discriminate science from non-science on the basis of the falsifiability of the scientific statements alone; he recognises that scientific theories are predictive, and consequently prohibitive, only when taken in conjunction with auxiliary hypotheses, and he also recognises that readjustment or modification of the latter is an integral part of scientific practice.」——辅助假说常需调整，且这是正当科学实践。
- 那么 ad hoc 的判别落点：「the only rationale for the modifications which were made to the original theory was to ensure that it evaded falsification, and so such modifications were ad hoc, rather than scientific.」⇒ 判据是**修改的唯一理由是不是为了逃避证伪**，而非「有没有修改」。「Marxism can no longer be dismissed as 'unscientific' simply because its advocates preserved the theory from falsification by modifying it」。
- 批判者指出这一收缩使「falsificationism ... fares no better in the final analysis than verificationism」——作为判据须标注其争议，不能当成已解决的科学定义。
- 与 §十六 认知接种、§十一 溯因 IBE、§十四 论证图式分工：本章管「一个主张/理论是否可被经验推翻、以及遇到反证时该改哪一层」；IBE 管「多个解释里选最佳」，认知接种管「预先给人抗说服能力」，论证图式管「短推定的举证责任」。四者不同命题，不互替。

## 判非（写结论时禁止的表述）
- 禁「该理论被证伪所以错」——Duhem-Quine 下失败检验只判定「一组前提里至少一处错」，需指明改的是哪一层。
- 禁「经受住检验所以被证实」——只能说 corroborated（暂获确证），「never verified」。
- 禁「有反例就该立刻放弃理论」——Popper 方法上明说「does not abandon the present theory until ... a better one to substitute」。
- 禁「改辅助假说=ad hoc 耍赖」——晚期立场承认调整辅助假说常是正当的，只有唯一动机是逃避证伪才算 ad hoc。
- 禁把证伪主义当成已无争议的科学划界定论——SEP §12 明确记录其收缩与批评。
