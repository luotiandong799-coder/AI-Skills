# r797A 阴性对照（Negative Control）检偏倚 — 逐字来源与判非

- 轮次：r797A（2026-11-23）· 模块 TI · 载体 `meta/ti-critical-reasoning`
- 主题：阴性对照结局/暴露（NCO/NCE）作为可经验证伪的偏倚探针；与 §22 E-value（量化混杂强度阈值）正交。

## 独点1｜NCO/NCE 因果结构：控制关联「必然虚假」是逻辑前提
一手（Penning de Vries & Groenwold, "Negative controls: Concepts and caveats", *Statistical Methods in Medical Research*, 2023, DOI 10.1177/09622802231181230, PMCID PMC10515451；WebFetch 200 全文逐字）：
> "A negative control outcome (NCO) is a variable that is not causally affected by the exposure of interest A. Likewise, a negative control exposure (NCE) is a variable that does not causally affect the outcome of interest Y, except possibly through the exposure of interest."
> "Given the absence of a direct causal effect of exposure A on an NCO Z or of NCE Z on outcome Y, any observed association between A and an Z, or between an Z and outcome Y given A, must be spurious. Leveraging negative controls involves translating information about such spurious associations into information about the spuriousness of associations between the primary exposure and outcome variables of interest."

判据：NCO 须与真实结局共享病因但不被暴露导致；NCE 须与暴露共享病因但不因果影响结局（除经暴露）。"控制关联必为虚假"是演绎前提，不是经验假设——先立结构，再拿控制关联当探针。Arnold 等（*Epidemiology* 2016;27(5):637-641, DOI 10.1097/EDE.0000000000000504，WebSearch 命中 BMJ/UC Berkeley eScholarship 逐字）补充："a negative control outcome acts as a surrogate for the actual outcome—the negative control should be subject to the same potential sources of bias as the outcome but is not caused by the exposure of interest."；并把结构从「未测混杂」扩到选择偏倚与测量偏倚："we define the structure of negative controls to detect selection bias and measurement bias in both observational studies and randomized trials."

## 独点2｜C 与 E 的方向决定解读，方向反了结论全反
一手（同上 PMC10515451 WebFetch 200 全文逐字）：
> "Let Y(a) denote the outcome that would be realized had exposure A been set to a... epidemiologists often seek to invoke the exchangeability (or unmeasured confounding) condition Y(a)⊥⊥A... A key idea of negative controls is to find a 'control' statement, C, that translates into information about E and which is more easily verified or refuted."
> "If C implies E, then a null finding for the control statement would imply conditional exchangeability for the exposure–outcome relation of interest. Conversely, if E implies C, evidence of the bias of the control association corroborates the existence of unmeasured confounding."

判据：只有「C⇒E」时控制关联为零才能推主关联可识别；只有「E⇒C」时控制关联有偏才能佐证混杂。两者是相反的蕴含方向，不声明 C/E 关系就解读零/非零结果等于把逻辑倒置。Lipsitch 的连接原则（同文转述）："based on the extent to which common causes of A and Y overlap with the common causes of the exposure or outcome and the negative control variable"——完全重叠时「A 与 Y 的共同原因集为空」当且仅当「A 与 NCO 的共同原因集为空」。

## 独点3｜缺特异性与敏感性：零结果不证无混杂，非零不证有混杂
一手（同上 WebFetch 200 全文逐字）：
> "A biased negative control association need not imply unmeasured confounding for the exposure–outcome relation of interest and neither is the converse true generally."
> "a (near) null finding could be the result of opposing biases, masking the presence of unmeasured confounding. In other words, negative controls are a tool that may lack both specificity and sensitivity with respect to the type(s) of bias they are to detect."
> "a spurious negative control association could have resulted, at least in part, from collider stratification, measurement error, or violations of assumptions about missing data."
> "With finite samples, proving the null hypothesis of a null negative control association is impossible."；须区分 "absence of evidence and evidence of absence"；"high power to detect 'small departures' from exposure-NCO ... need not imply high power to detect small bias due to unmeasured confounding of the primary relation"。

判据：碰撞分层/测量误差/缺失机制都能造出假阳性；反向偏倚可抵消成近零（假阴性）；必须报「控制关联偏倚与主关联偏倚的相对大小」而非只报一个 p 值。Arnold 2016 给选择偏倚四结构：C 是 A 与 Y、或 U_A 与 Y、或 A 与 U_Y、或 U_A 与 U_Y 的共同后代时条件于 C 即产生选择偏倚，NCO/NCE 须同样影响入组 C 才能探到。
