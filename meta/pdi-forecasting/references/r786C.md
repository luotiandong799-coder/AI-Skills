# r786C 全文下沉 · 多变量概率评分：energy score 与 variogram score

> 正文摘要见 `../SKILL.md` §二十三。本文件为逐字来源与完整推导存档，2026-11-20 实拉。

## 来源逐字

### ETH Zürich / CRAN scoringRules vignette《Weighted scoringRules》(arXiv:2305.07312)
- 动机逐字：While the LogS in Equation 1 can readily be applied to multivariate predictive densities, it is often the case that only a sample from the multivariate forecast distribution is available, making it difficult to employ the LogS in practice. Instead, alternative scoring rules have been proposed to evaluate multivariate probabilistic forecasts that can readily be applied to samples from a forecast distribution.
- Energy score（ES；Gneiting and Raftery 2007）逐字：Arguably the most well known multivariate scoring rule is the energy score, which generalises the CRPS to higher dimensions: `ES(F,y) = E_F‖X−y‖ − ½ E_F‖X−X′‖`，其中 ‖·‖ 是 R^d 欧氏距离，y=(y₁,…,y_d)∈Ω，X=(X₁,…,X_d)、X′=(X′₁,…,X′_d)~F 独立，F 为 Ω 上概率分布，期望在需要处有限。
- Variogram score（VS；Scheuerer and Hamill 2015）逐字：An alternative to the energy score is the variogram score, which aims to explicitly assess the dependence structure of the multivariate forecast distributions by measuring the distance between the variogram of the forecast and that of the observation. p 阶：`VS^p(F,y) = Σ_{i=1}^d Σ_{j=1}^d h_{i,j} (E_F|X_i−X_j|^p − |y_i−y_j|^p)^2`，h_{i,j}≥0 是控制某维对权重的非负标度参数；Following recommendations from Scheuerer and Hamill (2015), p is often chosen to be 0.5。
- 同属 kernel scores 逐字：Both the energy score and the variogram score belong to the very general class of kernel scores (Gneiting and Raftery 2007), constructed using conditionally negative definite kernels；同框架还有 inverse multiquadric kernel（Allen 2023b）与 Gaussian kernel 对应的 MMDS（maximum discrepancy score）：`MMDS(F,y)=½E_F[exp{−½‖X−X′‖²}] − E_F[exp{−½‖X−y‖²}]`。

### Springer《Evaluating Density Forecasts Using Weighted Multivariate Scores in a Risk Management Context》(10.1007/s10614-024-10571-y)
- LogS 局限逐字：the logarithmic score is not sensitive to distance, which means it only rewards the predictive densities for assigning high probabilities to realised values but not the neighbourhood values。
- ES 严格适当逐字：Gneiting and Raftery (2007) show that the energy score is strictly proper。带 β 形式 `ES_β(f,y)=E_f(‖X−y‖^β) − ½E_f(‖X−X′‖^β)`，β=1 为标准选择，通常 Monte Carlo 计算。
- ES 判别力局限逐字：Pinson and Tastu (2013) show that the discrimination ability of energy score may be limited while the dependence structure of multivariate probabilistic forecasts is misspecified。
- VS 适当性逐字：The variogram score is proper relative to the class of distributions for which the 2p-th moments of all elements are finite and it is not strictly proper (Scheuerer & Hamill, 2015)。p 是各成对偏差相对矩与离群点的权衡，典型 0.5 与 1。
- 加权警示逐字：Gneiting and Ranjan (2011) demonstrate that if proper scoring rules are weighted by a function that depends on the observed outcome, then the score is generally rendered improper；保适当的加权见 Diks 2011 / Holzmann & Klar 2017。

### jhschlegel《A Theoretical Primer on Proper Scoring Rules》(2026-01-30)
- ES 定义逐字同 ES(F,y)=E_F‖X−y‖^β−½E_F‖X−X′‖^β。
- VS 适当性逐字：For p∈(0,2) and non-negative weights, the Variogram Score is proper, though generally not strictly proper. It is sensitive to dependence through component-wise differences。
- 集成估计逐字：`VS^p(F_m,y)=Σ_{i=1}^dΣ_{j=1}^d w_{ij}(|y_i−y_j|^p − 1/m Σ_{k=1}^m |x_i^{(k)}−x_j^{(k)}|^p)^2`。
- 二者互补逐字：In practice, the Energy and Variogram Scores are complementary: the former captures overall distributional accuracy, while the latter more directly probes dependence。

### RMetS《Comparison of multivariate post-processing methods using global ECMWF ensemble forecasts》(10.1002/qj.4436)
- CRPS→ES 逐字：A multivariate extension of the CRPS is the energy score；对集成预报用经验 CDF（Gneiting 2008）得 ensemble energy score。
- VS 逐字：A more recently introduced multivariate proper scoring rule is the (ensemble) variogram score of order p；Compared with the ES, the VS is more sensitive to the errors in the specification of correlations。
- Skill score 逐字：skill score 正向（越大越好）`s = 1 − S(f)/S(f_ref)`（Gneiting and Raftery 2007）；DM 检验证显著性（Diebold and Mariano 1995）能计入预报误差的时间依赖，原假设等性能下 t 渐近标准正态，负值表示 f 更好。
