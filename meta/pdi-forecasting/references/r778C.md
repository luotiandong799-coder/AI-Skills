# r778C 补充全文与逐字证据（PDI · 2026-11-20）

## 一、评分规则不能判定可信（arXiv 2209.04872 §2.2）
> "Although proper scoring rules allow competing prediction systems to be ranked and compared objectively, they cannot be used to determine whether a prediction system is trustworthy, in the sense that the observed outcomes are statistically consistent with the forecasts that were issued. If the forecasts do align with the observations, then the prediction system is said to reliable, or calibrated."
Bröcker (2009) 的分解使 reliability 与 resolution 以相反符号进入严格适当分数，故一个方案的不可靠可被另一个的低分辨率抵消；任何单一聚合分都不能替代校准检查。

## 二、PIT / rank histogram 定义与局限（arXiv 2209.04872 §2.2；arXiv 2108.03210）
> "PIT histograms rely on the result that if the outcome Y is a continuous random variable with cumulative distribution function F, then F(Y) will follow the standard uniform distribution ... to check for calibration, we can evaluate each forecast distribution function at the observed outcome, F(y), and display these values in a histogram. If the observations are indeed draws from the corresponding forecast distributions, then the resulting histogram should be uniform. If the histogram is not uniform, then there is evidence to suggest the forecasts are miscalibrated, and the behaviour of the deviations can be used to diagnose the nature of the forecast errors."
> "independent, uniformly distributed PIT values do not imply auto-calibration, except in a special case. Thus, calibration diagnostics beyond checks of the uniformity and independence of the PIT are warranted."（2108.03210，England Bank CPI 案例）
形状诊断（玻尔百科/omni）：平坦=校准；U 形=区间太窄、过自信；驼峰=太宽、欠自信；倾斜=系统性偏差。PIT reliability diagram 可免分箱（2108.03210 S2.3）。

## 三、预测者困境用于诊断；cPIT（arXiv 2209.04872 §2.2）
> "The forecaster's dilemma also applies to diagnostic checks for calibration: if a rank or PIT histogram is constructed from only the forecasts issued when a high-impact event occurs, then the resulting histogram of a calibrated prediction system will in general not be uniform. For this reason, Bellier et al., (2017) strongly advise against observation-based stratification when constructing rank histograms."
> "let Y>t denote the conditional outcome variable given that the outcome exceeds a threshold t ... G(x)=[F(x)−F(t)]/[1−F(t)] for x>t, and G(x)=0 otherwise. The probability integral transform G(Y>t) then follows a standard uniform distribution."
> "These cPIT histograms are not equivalent to focusing on the bins on the right-hand side of the standard PIT histogram, since an extreme observation could correspond to a low PIT value F(y) if the forecast predicts more extreme events to occur with a high probability."

## 四、CORP / MCB / 分组损失（2108.03210；erotica 设计笔记）
- 传统 probabilistic / marginal calibration 是弱可靠性；若两者已 gross violations 则更强条件校准必违反，否则再查 threshold / mean / quantile calibration。
- CORP reliability diagram：PAV 保序、免分箱、90% consistency bands；CORP 分解 `BS = MCB - DSC + UNC`；MCB（≥0，零 iff 校准）替 ECE 作标量，DSC 分离「未校准」与「无区分力」。
- 分组损失 `GL(p)=Var[f*(X)|f(X)=p]` 是条件校准轴（Perez-Lebel 2023），与边际 ECE 正交。
- Hosmer–Lemeshow 在大样本把小偏离判显著且与分箱 ECE 同样不稳，弃用（Van Calster 2019；Paul/Pennell/Lemeshow 2013）；binned ECE 是总体校准误差下界（Kumar 2019 Prop 3.3）。

## 五、零样本/金融场景的结构性失准（omni 数据诊断）
- 校准=频率声明（需结果核验），锐度=有用性声明（无需结果）；目标是在保校准下最大锐度。
- 分层诊断四档：nominal-level 覆盖（按 horizon step 分开）/ PIT 直方图形状 / regime 条件覆盖（波动、月末、断点）/ 尾部专项覆盖（99 分位三十窗口估不出）。
- 零样本区间宽度来自预训练语料噪声；波动聚集 + 厚尾 + 结构断点三因使「平均正确」在承压期恰好失准。
