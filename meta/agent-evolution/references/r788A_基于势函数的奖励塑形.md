# r788A 下沉全文｜基于势函数的奖励塑形（PBRS）与策略不变性

> 主节：`meta/agent-evolution/SKILL.md` §30｜轮号 r788A｜2026-11-22 独立实拉｜≥2 独立域一手交叉
> 主题：稀疏奖励下加中间奖励而不改变最优策略——Ng/Harada/Russell (1999) 定理及其扩展。

## 一、独点1：PBRS 定理与望远镜抵消

**定理（Ng, Harada, Russell 1999）逐字**（arXiv2501.00989 Theorem 1）：

> "Given task T=⟨S,A,p,r,γ⟩ with optimal policy π*, then the task T̃ with reward function r̃(s,a)=r(s,a)+γ E_{s'~p} Φ(s') − Φ(s) (5) has the optimal policy π̃*=π*, and its optimal value functions satisfy Q̃*(s,a)=Q*(s,a)−Φ(s) (6), Ṽ*(s)=V*(s)−Φ(s) (7), for a bounded, but otherwise arbitrary function Φ:S→ℝ."

**望远镜抵消逐字**（同一来源）：

> "Intuitively, the form γΦ(s')−Φ(s) represents a discrete-time derivative along trajectories in the MDP. Including this shaping term in a trajectory's expected return, subsequent terms have a telescopic cancellation, leaving behind only Φ(s0)."

中文教程（Walking Labs）给出完整展开，逐字：

> "算一条轨迹 s0→s1→⋯→sT 上所有附加奖励的折扣和：∑γt[γΦ(s_{t+1})−Φ(s_t)]。展开后中间项全部抵消（望远镜求和）：γΦ(s1)−Φ(s0)、γ²Φ(s2)−γΦ(s1)、γ³Φ(s3)−γ²Φ(s2)…γTΦ(sT)−γ^{T−1}Φ(s_{T−1})，最后只剩 −Φ(s0)+γTΦ(sT)。终止状态势能设为 0，就只剩常数 −Φ(s0)——它只和起点有关，和走的路径无关。因此原来回报高的路径，加上这个常数后仍然更高，策略优劣次序不变。"

**非 PBRS 形式会改策略**（growbotics 场景，逐字）：奖励"抓住把手"的 bonus 会被"松开—再抓"反复领取："The policy soon learns to reach and grasp but then stalls, because releasing and regrasping the handle collects the grasp bonus again and again." 换成势函数差（沿状态递进才得分）后循环不再获利。glossary 定义亦逐字："shaping terms of a specific form, F(s,s′) = γΦ(s′) − Φ(s) ... leave the optimal policies of the original problem unchanged. Because any reward collected by moving through a cycle of states sums to zero under such terms, the agent cannot profit from loops that do not advance the task. Terms not of this form can change the optimal policy."

## 二、独点2：定理不指定 Φ；等价初始化与两种"动态"

**不给 preferred Φ 逐字**（arXiv2501.00989）：

> "The results of Theorem 1 show that the RL problem designer has the freedom to choose any Φ to shape the reward in a way that is consistent with the original MDP's solution. However, it importantly does not give any specific prescription for a 'preferred' choice of Φ:S→ℝ, which is up to the user to define."

**Φ=V* 的理想情形**（AAAI 论文逐字）：设 Φ(s)=V*(s) 时，仅凭即时塑形奖励贪心即为最优策略：

> "argmax_a Σ_{s'} T(s,a,s')[R(s,a,s')+γV*(s')−V*(s)] = argmax_a Σ_{s'} T(s,a,s')[R+γV*(s')] = argmax_a Q*(s,a) = π*(s)."

**距离势是特例**（Walking Labs 逐字）："取 Φ(s)=−d(s,goal)、γ=1，就有 F=d(s)−d(s′)。"

**Wiewiora 2003 等价性**（arXiv2501.00989 逐字）：

> "This result was further extended by Wiewiora 2003, who proved the equivalence between PBRS and Q-value initialization ... The previous proofs ... suggest that the shaping method used is equivalent to a particular Q-table initialization ... Q^(0)(s,a)=Φ^∞(s)=V*(s)/(1+η). However, the results in Wiewiora 2003 were only proven for static potentials which do not change over the course of training."

**Dynamic PBRS vs BSRS 的"动态"分叉**（arXiv2501.00989 逐字）：

> "Dynamic PBRS (Devlin and Kudenko 2012) extended PBRS to a dynamic setting where the potential function is time-dependent within the MDP. Here, 'time' refers to the transition step in the MDP itself, rather than the training step in the algorithm. ... On the other hand, we study a potential function Φ(s) that is fixed across time-steps in the MDP, but varies at each training step."

⇒ 两种"动态"含义不同：一个是 MDP 内部时间步变（仍收敛到最优），一个是算法训练步变；混报会导致收敛性结论张冠李戴。

## 三、独点3：能力边界与其它不变变换

- **只保策略不变，不保学得更快**（Walking Labs 逐字）："PBRS 只保证最优策略不变，不保证学得更快更稳。而且 PBRS 要求你能写出合理的势能函数 Φ(s)。迷宫里距离好算，但『这段对话进展如何』这类状态很难定义势能。"
- **更高的塑形回报 ≠ 更好策略**（growbotics Common Misconceptions 逐字）："A higher shaped return means a better policy — only performance on the true task objective shows whether the policy does what was intended." 训练时看塑形回报、验收必须回到真实稀疏成功判据。
- **线性变换的 horizon 边界**（ICAPS10-WS PDF 逐字）："policy invariance is guaranteed under any linear transformation r=pr+q, where p>0 ... In the case of an indefinite horizon problem ... the optimal policy may change, being attracted by (respectively avoiding) looping behaviors if q has a large positive (resp. negative) value." ⇒ PBRS 与简单线性缩放都保不变，但后者在不确定视界下会因常数 q 诱发循环/放弃；PBRS 的望远镜结构本身抵消了这个常数。
- **与既有技能分工**：§16 bandit 是"在线自适应如何分试验预算以最小 regret"、§23 安全探索是"学的过程受约束"、§28/§29 是"评估很贵/跨任务怎么学"；本章是"奖励信号怎么安全加密"——四者正交，不并存。判重：`reward shaping`/`potential-based`/`PBRS`/`策略不变性` 全库 SKILL.md 本体 0；豆包自留地 `wb-execute-discipline`/`wb-context-compressor` WB 0 落地。
