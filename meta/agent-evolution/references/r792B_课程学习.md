## 独点 1：一门课程 = 难度打分函数 + 进度函数两件；自步学习把「谁来定难度」内化，但会锁死难样本
- 一手逐字（Ultralytics glossary，WebFetch 200 全文）："A curriculum needs two components, which Hacohen and Weinshall call the **scoring function** and the **pacing function**. The scoring function ranks training data by difficulty, using human heuristics such as object size, occlusion, or sentence length, the confidence of a pretrained teacher model, or the loss of the model being trained. The pacing function decides how quickly harder examples join the pool the model trains on, for example starting with the easiest 20% and growing linearly to the full dataset."
- scoring 的三条来源路径：人工先验（物体大小/遮挡/句长）、教师模型置信度、模型当前 loss。pacing 控制进入训练池的速度；示例池从最易 20% 线性增长到全集，池均教师置信度从 0.99 降到 0.89。
- 自步学习（Self-Paced Learning，Kumar et al. 2010）："Self-paced learning removes the need for a teacher by letting the model build its own curriculum from its current loss … Each round, samples the model already handles well join the training pool, and the loss threshold rises until every sample is included."（Ultralytics）JMLR 21(18-751) 逐字给出其目标函数：训练 loss 与正则项联合最小化，正则项对「更契合当前假设（loss 更低）」的点加权更大 `v_i^* = max(0, 1 − ℓ_i/λ)`。
- **失败模式（两条独立来源互证）**：JMLR 逐字 "by relying only on the learner's training loss, the optimization is more susceptible to problems such as over-ﬁtting and training instability"；TheoremPath 逐字 "Self-paced learning can ignore hard but important examples indefinitely if λ increases too slowly. It can also create a feedback loop: the model never trains on hard examples, so they always have high loss, so they are never included." ⇒ 自步学习必须显式设阈值上升速度并抽查难样本是否长期缺席。

## 独点 2：全局难度 vs 局部难度解开「课程学习 vs 难例挖掘」的表面矛盾
- 一手（JMLR 21 18-751，WebSearch 全文片段逐字）：SPL「only relying on the learner's training loss … seems to contradict other commonly used heuristics, which attach greater signiﬁcance to points that do not ﬁt well … namely, incur higher loss. Examples include hard data mining (Shrivastava et al., 2016) and boosting (Schapire et al., 1998)」。
- 论文的解法是**两个难度分开定义**："we formally differentiate between the **global diﬃculty score** as deﬁned above [the loss at the optimal hypothesis w.r.t. the example], and the **local diﬃculty score** as deﬁned by the loss at a point with respect to the current hypothesis." 结论逐字："ideally a learner should follow a curriculum based on extrinsic (global) diﬃculty, while not 'wasting time' on examples that are easy for the current (local) learning hypothesis."
- 收敛率两侧（论文在两个凸问题——线性回归与 hinge loss 分类——上证明）："the convergence rate is expected to decrease monotonically with the diﬃculty of the sampled examples"；且 "when examples are drawn conditioned on some ﬁxed global diﬃculty score, the convergence rate … is monotonically increasing with the local diﬃculty of the example."
- 与 importance sampling 统一（TheoremPath 逐字）："Curriculum learning addresses non-convex optimization (finding good basins), while importance sampling addresses stochastic gradient variance (faster convergence within a basin)." 难例上权（OHEM/boosting，按 `p_i ∝ ‖∇ℓ_i‖`）降梯度方差、课程下权难例找好盆地，**两者解决不同问题，不是互相否定**。⇒ 落地判据：写「用了课程学习」须注明 scoring 用的是全局还是局部难度，否则与难例挖掘的取舍无法判断。

## 独点 3：收益比直觉少且集中在特定条件；窄课程烤进 shortcut；晋升规则要抗噪
- 反直觉实证（Ultralytics 逐字）："Curricula help less often than the intuition suggests. In thousands of training runs, Wu, Dyer, and Neyshabur found only marginal benefits on standard image benchmarks, where randomly ordered data in a growing pool did as well as an easy-to-hard order. Curricula did improve results when the training-time budget was limited or the labels were noisy, the settings where practitioners most often use them." ⇒ 先判条件（预算受限/标签有噪/RL 长时域），不满足就用随机增长池作对照，别默认课程更优。
- 窄课程风险（groundtruth.day 逐字）："A curriculum that spends the whole early phase on one kind of easy example can bake in a shortcut … where the model gets right answers for the wrong reason and then struggles when the shortcut stops working."；「决定什么算易」时 "difficulty for a human is not always difficulty for a model, and getting this wrong produces a curriculum that helps nothing."
- RL 里的晋升门（ai.kakuto.io 逐字）："Training statistics are noisy. A rule that looks at one iteration can promote on a lucky batch … Better rules: average over several iterations; wait after each promotion until the statistics come only from episodes at the new level; require both a low failure rate and long episodes." 实证：单迭代规则 15 步从 0.2 冲到满幅（用旧关卡数据判的），加「每次晋升后等两个 episode 长度」才稳定。
- 概念分家（Ultralytics 逐字）：continual learning 加新任务且要防遗忘、active learning 挑未标注去标、transfer learning 任务本身在阶段间变化；课程学习只重排**单一固定任务**的数据。hard negative mining 与 easy-first 是反方向。⇒ 验收四问：scoring 函数、pacing 函数、晋升是否多迭代平均+换关后等待、与随机池对照是否真的赢。
