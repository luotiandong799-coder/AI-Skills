## 一手来源（逐字 + URL + 日期）

1. Wikipedia《Stochastic dominance》（en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_dominance，WebFetch 200 全文，访问 2026-11-23）：
   - "Stochastic dominance is a partial order between random variables... one gamble... can be ranked as superior to another gamble for a broad class of decision-makers. Only limited knowledge of preferences is required for determining dominance. Risk aversion is a factor only in second order stochastic dominance."
   - "Stochastic dominance does not give a total order, but rather only a partial order: for some pairs of gambles, neither one stochastically dominates the other."
   - FSD："A gives at least as high a probability of receiving at least x as does B... for all x, F_A(x) ≤ F_B(x)." 等价定义："a gamble first-order stochastically dominates... if and only if every expected utility maximizer with an increasing utility function prefers" A over B。
   - 序关系："if A ≽₁ B, then A ≽₂ B... there exists... but not" 反向（低阶蕴含高阶，反之不然）。

2. MDPI Mathematics 11(1):50《Comparing SSD-Efficient Portfolios with a Skewed Reference Distribution》（mdpi.com/2227-7390/11/1/50/xml，WebFetch 返回空 Title 页；正文取自 WebSearch 逐字快照，2026-11-23 访问，未冒充全文）：
   - Definition 1 FSD："X FSD-dominates Y iff F_X(α) ≤ F_Y(α) ∀α∈R, or E[u(X)]≥E[u(Y)] ∀u∈U₁, where U₁ denotes the class of all nondecreasing utility functions."
   - Definition 2 SSD："∫_{−∞}^α F_X(t)dt ≤ ∫_{−∞}^α F_Y(t)dt ∀α∈R, or E[u(X)]≥E[u(Y)] ∀u∈U₂, where U₂ indicates the class of all nondecreasing and concave utility functions."
   - 尾部等价："X SSD-dominates Y iff Tail_β(X) ≥ Tail_β(Y) ∀β∈(0,1], where Tail_β(X) represents the unconditional expectation of the worst β·100% of the outcomes."；"X SSD-dominates Y iff E[max(α−X,0)] ≤ E[max(α−Y,0)] ∀α∈R."
   - "as the order increases, the requirements of... SD condition become less restrictive... a stochastic dominance of the lower orders implies a stochastic dominance of the higher orders, while the opposite is not necessarily true."

3. University of Manchester EDP-1123《Risk Behaviour for Gain, Loss and Mixed Prospects》（hummedia.manchester.ac.uk/.../EDP-1123.pdf，WebFetch 200 全文，2026-11-23）：
   - "FSD is a simple criterion, people agree with this principle and apply it in simple situations. Though, in nontransparent choice situations many violate this dominance criterion."
   - "SSD is less strict... requires that, if ∫[F_P(t)−F_Q(t)]dt ≤ 0 for all outcomes x with at least one inequality being strict, P is preferred to Q... Q assigns more probability mass to at least one lower ranked outcome and does not sufficiently compensate by assigning more probability mass to better ranked outcomes, Q is regarded as more risky."
   - 对前景理论的边界："Schmidt and Zank showed that SSD requires that utility is concave for gains and concave for losses but not necessarily concave at the reference point... the theoretical implications of SSD for PT do not fit well with the empirical evidence, which suggests that utility is concave for gains but convex for losses and that the weighting functions have an inverse-S shape."

4. 百度百科《随机优势》（baike.baidu.com/subview/4941197/4942603.htm，WebFetch 200 全文，2026-11-23）：
   - "A 一阶随机占优于 B——在所有偏好多而厌恶少的个体看来，A 优于 B；A 二阶随机占优于 B——在所有风险厌恶的个体看来，A 优于 B；...三阶...风险厌恶而且绝对风险厌恶递减的个体看来."
   - "与经典的均值-方差准则相比，随机优势规则在选择互斥投资时具有更广泛的适用性，但在识别所有 SSD 有效的分散化策略方面则更为复杂...均值-基尼模型能找到 SSD 有效集的一个子集，但它仅是 SSD 的必要而非充分条件。"

5. 聚宽《为什么是波动率?》（joinquant.com/view/community/detail/c65827ad98eacd855fbc47927c4ae7cf，WebFetch 200 全文，2026-11-23）：
   - "第一阶随机优势：如果投资组合A在所有可能收益水平下的累积概率都优于B...那么所有风险厌恶者都会偏好A。第二阶随机优势：如果A的分布比B的分布'更少分散'（即风险厌恶者的效用函数是凹的），那么A占优。在收益呈正态分布的假设下，第二阶随机优势准则与均值-方差分析是完全一致的。"

## 判非（不采 / 边界）

- 不把 SSD 当全序排序器：Wikipedia 明言偏序、存在互不可比的成对赌局；给不出唯一最优是合法输出。
- 不声称 SSD 对所有理论都成立：Manchester 论文给出 SSD 对 PT 的理论要求与实证不符（loss 侧凸 vs 要求凹、inverse-S 权重 vs 要求 gain 凸/loss 凹），故 SSD 结论属 EU/RDU 框架，不直接外推到前景理论描述场景。
- 不把"正态下 SSD≡均值-方差"当通则：聚宽与百度百科都指明这是正态假设下的特例；非正态时 MV 可能误导。
- 三阶 TSD 只在"绝对风险厌恶递减"附加假设下成立（百度百科），本条只落 FSD/SSD，TSD 留接口不展开。
- 统计检验（Barrett-Donald 2003）需样本与分布假设核查，本条只给判据不给检验实现。
