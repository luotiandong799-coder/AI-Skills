# r809B｜德尔菲法：无可信概率时如何把专家判断收敛成可审计的群体结论（DI · 2026-11-26）

> 正文下沉自 `meta/di-decision-tradeoff/SKILL.md` §三十二（r809B 实拉）。≥2 独立域一手交叉，URL+日期逐条。

## 一、三个区分特征（RAND《The Delphi Technique》逐字）

标题：THE DELPHI TECHNIQUE（NSA approved for release 12-01-2011, Transparency Case# 63852；media.defense.gov PDF）。原文逐字：

> "Three features distinguish the Delphi technique from a simple round-table or panel technique: 1. Anonymous response, 2. Iteration and controlled feedback, and 3. Statistical derivation of a group response."

匿名要避的四类问题（逐字）：① 「A group may be persuaded by individuals who dominate by virtue of rank, reputation, or (sheer) personality.」② 「A member of a group may fear 'losing face' by abandoning a publicly expressed opinion.」③ 「The 'bandwagon' effect often leads to adoption of solutions that are less than optimal.」④ 「Group discussions generate large amounts of 'noise', irrelevant discussion and sidetracks that contribute nothing to the central issue.」
迭代机制（逐字）：「Responses to the first set of questions are tabulated and sent to the participants, who are asked to respond again, using the information provided by the other responses. This feedback-and-response cycle may continue through several iterations.」并自陈风险「the persons tabulating responses and coordinating the Delphi exercise can consciously or accidentally distort the feedback and bias the results.」
统计聚合（逐字）：「production of a single group response rather than simply a tabulation of individual responses... aggregated in a manner that assures that every opinion contributes to the final group opinion.」

## 二、四原则 + 共识阈值（Lotus / survey.org / Wikipedia 逐字）

- 四原则（Lotus 逐字）：「Anonymity / Iteration / Controlled feedback / Statistical aggregation — final consensus is quantified using medians, interquartile ranges, or percentage agreement.」
- 专家规模（Lotus 逐字）：「Recruit **10–30 experts** (Okoli & Pawlowski, 2004). Larger panels increase reliability but reduce response rates.」「typically 3–4 rounds over 2–3 months」；Round 1 开放生成 → Round 2 Likert 1–9 评分算 median/IQR → Round 3 匿名群体统计回灌重评 → Round 4 可选。
- 共识阈值（须事前定义，Lotus 逐字）：「≥70% agreement within a predefined range (e.g., scores 7–9 = 'important').」「IQR ≤ 2 indicates acceptable agreement.」「Items not reaching consensus after 3 rounds are classified as 'no consensus.'」
- CREDES 报告规范（Jünger et al. 2017，Lotus 逐字）：「Report response rates per round and reasons for dropout.」「Present consensus and non-consensus items transparently.」「Include stability analysis (did ratings change significantly between rounds?).」「Discuss limitations: selection bias, panel composition, and the difference between consensus and truth.」
- 偏差值处理（ArabPsychology Encyclopedia 逐字）：极端值者「required to provide a written justification for their minority or extreme position」，理由匿名传播「allows experts to be persuaded by logical arguments rather than social pressure」。
- 变体（Lotus 逐字）：Modified Delphi「starts with a predefined list (skips open Round 1)」；Real-time Delphi「Web-based platform allows asynchronous, continuous updating」；Policy Delphi「seeks to explore disagreement, not force consensus」。

## 三、判据（三条独点）

### 独点1：四特征缺一不可，去掉匿名就不是德尔菲法
- 判据：① 「大家开会讨论到一致」不是德尔菲——无匿名则权威压制/怕丢面子/从众三偏倚全在。② 去掉可控反馈只剩多轮问卷，去掉统计聚合只剩定性归纳；四特征要在方法说明里逐条落地。③ 回灌的统计表由协调者编制 ⇒ 必须固化「回灌内容生成规则」并留痕，防协调者无意识扭曲（RAND 自陈风险）。

### 独点2：共识 ≠ 正确；阈值须事前冻结，未达即报「no consensus」不硬凑
- Wikipedia 逐字：「reaching consensus does not necessarily show that the correct answer has been found but only that agreement has been reached among the experts.」
- 判据：① 阈值（70%/IQR≤2/轮数）在发第一轮问卷前写死，事后改阈值=操纵结论。② 三轮未达共识按「no consensus」如实上报，与 §r736B 候选项冻结同构——都是防「调规则凑结果」。③ 报告附每轮回应率与退出原因、稳定性分析、panel 组成局限；共识条目与未共识条目并列呈现。

### 独点3：专家排序/自评是陷阱；与投票悖论节分工在「收什么」
- RAND Critique 逐字：「ranking the experts may be as difficult as making the original decision, and attempts to use self-ratings by each expert may measure ego as much as expertise.」并「the choice of experts is crucial, in both the area of choosing general classes and the area of naming specific names.」
- 分工：§二十九投票/社会选择（r736B）管「已有偏好如何聚合、聚合规则本身有哪些定理级缺陷」——收完整排序/成对比较、冻结候选集；本节点管「偏好本身还没成形、需要从Experts激发并收敛」——收匿名评分+理由、冻结阈值。两者同属群体决策但前置条件不同：前者偏好既存，后者偏好待生成。
- 判据：① 选人标准（专业/经验/视角多样/地域）先于问卷设计，不用自评排序加权。② 需要「探索分歧而非强制一致」时明选 Policy Delphi，不拿 consensus 率当成功指标。③ 与 §20 无概率准则分工：minimax regret 是「已知选项+未知概率」的准则，德尔菲是「连选项/判断都待生成」的收敛流程，先用德尔菲生成并收敛判断，再进入 minimax regret/EVPI 等准则。
