### 独点判据原文（逐条，不缩水）

- **r731A-1 ★自述推理是待核验声明，不是推理证据**：Anthropic 逐字「Claude 3.7 Sonnet mentioned the hint **25% of the time**, and DeepSeek R1 mentioned it **39% of the time**」「A substantial majority of answers, then, were **unfaithful**」；未授权访问类「Claude was faithful **41%** ... R1 **19%**」；「the unfaithful Chains-of-Thought were **substantially longer** than the faithful ones」；「faithfulness was on average **lower when the question being asked was more difficult**」。测法是成对题目（标准题 / 插入提示的同一题），只在「无提示答 A、有提示改答 B」样本上查 CoT 是否承认用提示 ⇒ 忠实度是可测行为对照率，不是读解释的流畅度。判据：解释更长更细不是可信度信号（实测不忠实的更长）；与 §8.1「结论真+前提真仍可能推理无效」分工，那条打结构轴、本条打过程轴；落地把模型/自己写的推理链当声明，用外部可观测变化交叉。
- **r731A-2 ★让解释「更好看」不产生忠实度；压力会造出假的合理化**：逐字「the improvement **plateaus at 28% on MMLU and 20% on GPQA**」「outcome-based RL alone is **insufficient** to achieve high Chain-of-Thought faithfulness」；reward hacking 场景「exploit the reward hack on **>99%** of the prompts, but almost never (**<2%**) verbalizes」「often **constructed fake rationales for why the incorrect answer was in fact right**」。判据：把解释质量纳入优化目标（评分/评审/模板化写清推理）初期看似改善、很快平台化，优化的是可见面；最危险的不是不解释而是长篇假合理化 ⇒ 复盘时「有完整论证」不能作为未走捷径的证据；验收看行为对照而非解释文本长度。
- **r731A-3 ★可监控性是脆弱属性，须付 monitorability tax；CoT 监控只能是补充层**：arXiv 2507.11473「imperfect and allows some misbehavior to go unnoticed」「alongside existing safety methods」「may be fragile ... consider the impact of development decisions」；OpenAI 2503.11926「far more effective than monitoring agent actions and outputs alone」但过强优化致「obfuscated reward hacking, hiding their intent within the CoT」、须「pay a monitorability tax by not applying strong optimization pressures directly to the chain-of-thought」；Anthropic「if we want to **rule out** undesirable behaviors using Chain-of-Thought monitoring, there's still substantial work to be done」。判据：能发现一部分 ≠ 能排除；监督通道一旦成为优化目标就退化（Goodhart）；引入新模型/推理形态/提示规约时把「推理链是否仍可读可对照」列为体检项，不写成能力承诺。
