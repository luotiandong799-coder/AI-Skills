## 三、本轮独有点（判重净新）

1. §八 r742B-1 claimed length ≠ effective length 且差距是常态：Compile N Run 逐字「A config.json that says max_position_embeddings: 131072 tells you the largest position the model will accept. It does not tell you whether the model can find, use or reason over a fact that sits 100,000 tokens back」；RULER 定义 effective context length＝13 任务均分保持在 Llama2-7B@4K（85.6%）阈值之上的最长长度，实测「Almost every model passes vanilla NIAH at its claimed length and fails RULER's threshold well before it」：GPT-4 128K→64K、Qwen2 72B 128K→32K；Futurecraft 独立逐字「only half of them can maintain satisfactory performance at the length of 32K」与 NoLiMa 有效长度表（GPT-4o 128K→8K、Gemini 2.0 Flash 1M→4K，16x/250x）。⇒ 预算按有效长度定并标型号/基准/日期，「窗口没满」不构成安全声明。
2. §八 r742B-2 绿色 NIAH 热力图只证明两件事：Compile N Run 逐字「A green heatmap shows that positions work and copying works, nothing more. Add distractors, multi-hop and non-literal variants」；HELMET（ICLR 2025）逐字 59 模型「NIAH-style synthetic tasks do not reliably predict downstream performance」「categories correlate weakly with each other」「open models lag closed ones most on tasks requiring full-context reasoning」；NoLiMa 去词面重叠后 13 个声称 128K+ 模型在 32K 有 11 个跌破自身短基线 50%、GPT-4o 99.3%→69.7%（Futurecraft 逐字交叉）⇒ 基准按「它抓什么」分记（NIAH 位置/复制、LitM 位置偏置、RULER 干扰项·多跳·聚合·QA、NoLiMa 关键词依赖、HELMET 应用级下游），不得用单针通过率给跨段/无字面重叠需求背书。
3. §八 r742B-3 评估有固定七步顺序且配置错误会伪装成能力退化：短上下文回归(≤4K)→困惑度仅 sanity check（「A blow-up means the configuration is wrong; a flat curve proves little」）→NIAH 网格（「Anything less than near-perfect here usually means a configuration error」）→RULER 按业务阈值算有效长度→自家任务位置扫描→语义非字面/应用级套件→成本延迟；Common Mistakes 逐字「Equating claimed and effective length」「Reporting only vanilla NIAH」「Using the chat model without its chat template… looks like a context failure but is not」「Forgetting to enable scaling at inference… measures extrapolation, not the advertised capability」「build haystacks by token count, not by characters」⇒ 长上下文掉分先排三个非能力因；Futurecraft 代际边界「You can't carry its numbers over to current… no comparable published tables」⇒ 本轮数值只作方法示例、落地以自测为准。
