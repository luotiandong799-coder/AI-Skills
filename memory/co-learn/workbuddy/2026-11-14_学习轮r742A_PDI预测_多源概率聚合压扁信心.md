## 三、本轮独有点（判重净新）

1. §十二 r742A-1 两个彼此独立的压扁机制，只有一个能靠换统计量躲开：Baron 等官方摘要逐字「The first factor is the compression of the probability scale at the two ends, so that random error tends to push the average probability toward 0.5. This effect does not occur for the median forecast, or, arguably, for the mean of the log odds」——这是算术平均自带的偏差；第二因素「forecasters taking into account their individual ignorance of the total body of information… fails to take into account the wisdom of crowds」对 mean/median/log-odds 均值三者都生效。官方「same transformation function…with different parameters for the mean and the median」「use of the median can help distinguish the two effects」⇒ 报聚合数写明统计量与变换、两成因参数不同、用中位数与均值之差做诊断。
2. §十二 r742A-2 极端化强度是信息重叠度的函数不是可调超参：arXiv 1406.2148 逐字「the optimal amount of extremizing of the average probability forecast…varies as a function of the experts' information overlap」「a more principled understanding of the historically ad hoc practice」；UQAM 逐字 linear opinion pools「necessarily uncalibrated and lacking in sharpness…leading to underconfidence」⇒ 信息完全重叠→平均、不重叠→投票；同一模型跑 5 次不是 5 个独立源；系数 a 必须从已结算同类题学出（log-odds 均值乘 a 映回概率，a=1 即赔率几何平均、a>1 才极端化），禁止事后挑让 Brier 好看的 a；登记每源信息集防重复计数。
3. §十二 r742A-3 聚合器选型按参数个数与样本外表现：IJF 30(2):344-356 逐字单参数「simple analytical form…robust against overfitting」、>1300 专家/69 事件「superior to several widely-used aggregation algorithms」；UQAM 逐字优于 unweighted mean 与 beta-transformed linear pool；DataField 逐字「provides both bias correction and extremization simultaneously」与 Forecast Combination Puzzle「the estimation error in optimal weights is often large enough to offset the theoretical advantage of optimization」⇒ 偏差校正与极端化分两栏记账、方案升级比样本外、简单平均是强基线。

- 判重：`extremiz` 全库 SKILL.md 仅 1 命中＝本技能 §三一句技法口号（无机制/边界/验收），本轮展开为两成因+重叠度函数+聚合器选型，不重复口号；`Ranjan` 命中在 `engineering/wb-execute-discipline`（人名 Ranjan Kumar，非 Ranjan & Gneiting 2010），不同命题。`log odds` / `logit aggregat` / `information overlap` / `wisdom of crowds` / `opinion pool` / `linear pool` / `under-confident` / `1300 experts` / `calibrated forecasts` 全库 SKILL.md **0 命中** ⇒ 净新。豆包自留地 WB 0 落地。
