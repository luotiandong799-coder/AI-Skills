## 关键原文摘录（可核验）

- 「**grade the outcome in the environment, not the claim in the transcript**」——只看会话文本的评测会大量假阳性，应查环境里的真实状态（退款表）。
- 「It might be tempting to create a single judge that rates accuracy, tone of voice, and completeness together on a 1-10 scale. This is sometimes also called a **God Evaluator**. The problem with this is that your resulting score does not tell you what to fix.」
- 「Suppose the failure you are checking for occurs in 10% of cases. A judge that answers _pass_ every time agrees with you 90% of the time… but actually tells you nothing.」
- 「Take 10 to 20 real cases of the failure mode you want to evaluate and label each one… _criteria drift_: you need criteria to grade outputs, but grading outputs is what teaches you your criteria.」
- 「**Keep dynamic values out of names.** Use `process-order`, not `process-order-8945`」；「Try not to name observations after the AI model used」。
- 「**One generation wrapping the whole loop.** You only see aggregated cost and the final output.」
- 「tags are **immutable and must be set at observation creation time**… If you need to label traces based on something determined after the fact… use scores instead.」
- 错误分析五步：Gather traces → **Open coding**（先不定类目）→ Cluster → Label and measure → Decide and act；「the first taxonomy is never the final one」。
- 数据集：15–30 行最小完备起步；「A regression dataset may intentionally overrepresent failures, edge cases, or high-value paths」；回归集与对抗集用途不同则拆。
