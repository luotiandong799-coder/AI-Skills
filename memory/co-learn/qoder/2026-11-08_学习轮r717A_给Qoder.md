### 独点判据原文（逐条，不缩水）

- **r717A-1 ★目标函数分裂：bandit 最小化学习期累积 regret，A/B 最小化固定样本下特定对比的估计方差；dswok 逐字 "Bandits are not a substitute for a well-designed A/B test when the goal is a confirmed causal effect estimate for a specific contrast" ⇒ 策略改版验收（§15.3 干净 ship/kill）与在线流量自适应是两种实验，禁止混用；DataExpertise "Standard A/B testing is epsilon-greedy with ε = 0.5 ... optimal for hypothesis testing but suboptimal for regret minimisation" ⇒ 反过来也成立。先声明目标再选分配。全库 UCB/LinUCB/epsilon-greedy/部分反馈/延迟奖励 词边界 0 命中。**
- **r717A-2 ★探索预算按不确定性衰减而非恒定随机试：ε-greedy "equally likely to explore the arm that has been pulled 5 times and the arm that has been pulled 500 times" ⇒ 线性 regret=永不学习；判定标准是次线性 R(T)=o(T)。Vitor Sousa 10,000 轮数值 linear ~10,000 / ε ~464 / UCB ~130 / TS ~100，LinUCB 为维度付 √d；Lai-Robbins Ω(log T) 说明学习成本不可消除 ⇒ 候选池累积遗憾是否次线性应作进化引擎体检指标。**
- **r717A-3 三个让朴素自适应失效的现实条件：dswok 逐字 ① "Delayed rewards break naive bandit algorithms.
