## 三、本轮独有点（判重净新）

1. §十二 r742C-1 结果偏差是独立偏差不是后见之明的别名：Identec 逐字「Outcome bias occurs when people judge a decision primarily by its result rather than by the information and reasoning available at the time」「good decisions do not always produce good outcomes, and poor decisions do not always produce bad outcomes」；两支海上维护队同样信息同样流程、一队平安一队伤人却只判后者决策有缺陷；官方「A robust incident analysis evaluates the quality of decisions independently of their outcomes」；Thinking Bugs 引 Baron & Hershey 1988 逐字「identical decisions were rated differently once people learned good versus bad outcomes… outcome knowledge systematically shifted evaluations」，并明确「Hindsight makes the past feel more predictable. Outcome bias uses the known result to grade whether the decision was competent」⇒ 模板强制两栏、结果不回写决策质量栏、结果翻转测试、两本账（quality of choice / new information from the result）、高风险决策结果到达前先落理由与概率。
2. §十二 r742C-2 三偏差各需不同纠正动作且不可互相顶替：hindsight→「reconstruct events chronologically and evaluate decisions using only information available before the incident occurred」；outcome→决策与结果独立评；fundamental attribution error→「overemphasise personal characteristics…while underestimating situational and organisational influences」并给六项系统因（workload/staffing/procedures/equipment/production pressures/organizational norms）；三者合流逐字造出「The hazard was obvious. The decision was poor. The individual caused the problem.」，后果「retraining an employee may have little impact if the real issue involves procedural complexity…」；Cybermaniacs 独立交叉「Hindsight bias harms incident learning in two ways…shallow corrective action」⇒ 模板三列（事前可知性/结果是否影响评级/是否只归个人）任一空即不完整；行动项只有再培训/提醒/纪律触发归因门；时间线纪律只解 hindsight。
3. §十二 r742C-3 技术时间线回答不了「系统为何有能力这样做」：Cybermaniacs 逐字「They may not explain why the system was capable of doing it. That requires a decision timeline」＋12 问（为何批准/哪些假设/谁接受剩余风险/何时用途越过原批准）；「A bare approval tells investigators that someone clicked a button. A concise rationale shows what they believed they were approving.」；NIST AI RMF「document system objectives, application scope, expected benefits and costs, human oversight, knowledge limits, and risks or impacts」；决策环境十一项。无事故样本：决策质量九项清单（含 defined conditions for reassessment）＋「many unsafe decisions will initially produce useful results…The absence of immediate harm becomes evidence that the decision was good. This is how weak decisions gain legitimacy.」；Thinking Bugs「unexamined wins teach luck as skill」「hide the final score until the process is graded」⇒ 抽样复盘「成功但绕过控制」、理由字段作 cognitive forcing function、决策记录含触发重评估条件、评过程先隐藏结果。
